The Connected Family
many devices, one happy network
With 18 devices under one roof — phones, tablets, consoles, and a small army of smart-home gadgets — yours is one of the busiest networks we manage. Smart Home / IoT alone was 20% of traffic. And yet it stayed fast and tidy all month: no congestion, no slowdowns, even on weekend evenings when everything is on at once.

The archetype is a compliment, never a diagnosis.
