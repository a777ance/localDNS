Spend bronze sparingly. It marks the operator's name, the rule under the header, the edge of a called-out block — the places where a human hand touched the work. Decorate with it and it stops meaning anything.

Never carry state by color alone. Every status on a Statement is also spelled out in words — “Needs attention”, “High”, “Healthy (17)”. These documents get printed, photocopied, and read by people who don't see red and green apart.
